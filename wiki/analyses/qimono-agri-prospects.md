# Agricultural prospects: Pampas × Australian grain belt

Market hypothesis, not existing clients or demonstrated interest. Biological fixation in soybean/pulses, synthetic ammonia production, and farm optimization are distinct value chains. Start with discovery, not a promise of a quantum nitrogenase solution. Australia's southern grain belt and Riverina share broadacre farming and legume-rotation opportunities with the Pampas, but climate, soils and crop mix differ.

GRDC consortium announced A$17.33M in 2024 for enhanced efficiency fertilizer; not quantum research. These establish industrial interest and scale, not willingness to purchase Qimono services. Los Grobo deprioritized pending credit and restructuring due diligence ([CNV](https://www.cnv.gov.ar/SitioWeb/Empresas/Empresa/30604456475?fdesde=18%2F9%2F2019), [rating](https://www.fixscr.com/reportes-web/view?id=60829)).

For farm customers use logistics/optimization/security first; for chemistry/biological suppliers use molecular or process research only with domain partner.
