## Notes

- The official Week 1 order is the lessons in `week2.md` first, then the Shor deep dive in `week1.md`. Inside raw week 1 the deep dive comes before the demonstrations. That matches the outline.
- The official Week 2 outline names five topics: post-quantum crypto, single-photon schemes, entangled-photon schemes, random number generators and quantum repeaters. They all map to raw week 3. Teleportation sits inside the repeaters unit.
- The official Week 3 outline says the Hamiltonian-simulation week includes a 30-min "Case Study: VQE in Practice". In the raw file, that is the [VQE in practice](../sources/week-4-07-vqe-in-practice.md) page.
