# Ekert 91 and BBM92

Ekert's protocol (E91) distributes a key from entangled pairs rather than from states Alice prepared. A source sends one qubit of a Bell pair to Alice and the other to Bob. They measure in randomly chosen bases. The bits from matching bases become the raw key. The bits from mismatched bases are used to estimate a [[concepts/bell-inequality]] violation, which certifies that the correlations were not produced by an eavesdropper who holds a classical description of both qubits.

BBM92 (Bennett, Brassard, Mermin) is the entanglement version of [[concepts/bb84]]: the same two bases, without the full CHSH estimation, relying on the monogamy of entanglement plus the sampled error rate.
